## The plank

`bots/_v198feederfirst` = incumbent `_v197mapcode` + `LOKI_FEEDER_FIRST`:
in `_turret`'s sentinel priority scan, an enemy BUILDER_BOT within
`LOKI_FEEDER_NEAR_DSQ=8` of a visible enemy gunner/sentinel is priority 0.5

treatment null HARDER to excuse, not easier.

on maps `midgard` and `frostgate` (one open 900-area, one small), 8 games/arm/map.
