⚠ **ONE THING PINNING DOES NOT DO: it does not pin OUR side.** `match unrated`

### 7.1 The metronome, and why exposure is arithmetic

Measured over **234 pairings / 77.7 h** (`FINDING-pairing-metronome-and-exposure-
cost-2026-08-15.md`): slots at **`:12:59`, `:32:59`, `:52:59` UTC**, gaps

last 19 h, **and we are paired in 100% of slots** (fill 3.01/hr against a
theoretical 3.00).

```
leaked_rated_matches  =  the number of :12:59 / :32:59 / :52:59 slots
                         between activation and rollback
```

**DETERMINISTIC, NOT PROBABILISTIC**, and computable before the command is typed.
**Each leaked match costs a measured mean of −5.94 Elo** (7 non-incumbent matches,
range −9.35 to −4.75; `CLAUDE.md`'s −8 budget is conservative in the right
direction and both are quoted so neither travels alone). ⚠ **The 100% fill and
the 19m58s of clear air are US-ONLY samples** — facts about how the ladder has
treated us for 77.7 h, not documented engine guarantees.

**⛔ RE-DERIVE THE PHASE, NEVER HARDCODE IT.** Read the last pairing off
`corpus/ladder_games.tsv` (or `fcode match list --mine --type ladder --limit 60`)
**on the day of firing**, confirm minute ≡ 12 (mod 20), second `:59`, and **refuse
