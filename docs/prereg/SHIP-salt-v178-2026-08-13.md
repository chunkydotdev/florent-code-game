# AMENDMENT 1 — 2026-08-13T04:5xZ. **THE STOP-LOSS NAMED THE WRONG STATISTIC.**

**ADD-ONLY. This amendment adds a constraint and loosens nothing.** Raised by the
side lane while the gate was still ~2.7h from arming (it arms at k>=8 rated

## THE DEFECT

The bar above says *"cumulative net Elo <= -21 (the standing slot-swap rule)."*
**Those are two different statistics and the parenthetical is false.** Read at
`tools/slot_rule.py:125-131`:

    base5 = None
    for _, r, m in rows:
        if m <= matches - WINDOW: base5 = r
    net5 = None if base5 is None else rating - base5
    slot_free = bool(armed and net5 is not None and net5 <= SWAP_THRESHOLD)

**The standing rule is a ROLLING FIVE-MATCH SLOPE.** `ship_watch` prints exactly
that as `net5=`. I pre-registered a cumulative bar, called it the standing rule,
and **nothing in the tree computes the thing I wrote.**

## WHY IT MATTERS HERE SPECIFICALLY, AND IT IS NOT PEDANTRY

`tools/monitors/ship_watch.py:124-130`, already in the tree and measured:

> *"a steady bleed slower than -4.2/match holds net5 above -21 forever — measured,
> a -4.0 bleed loses 240 Elo over 60 matches and trips neither this rule nor the
> SPRT."*

**SALT's characteristic failure is precisely a slow bleed, not a catastrophe.**
It is purely additive behind `LOKI_SALT_ON` and sits BELOW the existing raid
priorities, so it cannot produce a cliff — it can only quietly cost tempo on
rounds the parent used better. **The rule I actually armed is blind to the only
realistic way this ship is wrong.**

⇒ The two also disagree in the OTHER direction: for a true-zero holder, rolling-5
fires on noise at **14.1% by k=8 and 60.4% by k=20**
(`docs/research/SPEC-slot-rule-base-rate-2026-08-12.md`, live as `p_null` on the
`ship_watch` line). **So the automated rule is simultaneously trigger-happy on
noise and blind to a bleed.**

## THE AMENDED BAR — ROLL BACK IF **EITHER** FIRES

1. **`net5 <= -21` at `k >= 8`** — the automated rule, which is what will actually
   fire. **Its false-positive rate is NOT negligible and must be quoted at
   read-out: `p_null` from the `ship_watch` line.** A fire here is a trigger to
   LOOK, and the look is at the level columns, not a verdict on its own.
2. **Cumulative net Elo since v122's activation `<= -21` at `k >= 8`** — the level
   rule, which is the one that can see a bleed. **⛔ NOTHING COMPUTES THIS TODAY**
   — that is the honest state, and it is why this is written as an owed column
   rather than a satisfied bar. The ground truth is per-match `ourver` in
   `ladder_games.tsv` summed from the activation timestamp **04:45:54Z**; the
   nearest live proxy is `ship_watch`'s `drawdown` **with `dd_z`/`dd_k`**, which
   is peak-to-current and therefore NOT the same quantity.
3. **Unchanged and still immediate at any n:** rollback on a crash/timeout
   regression.

**⚠ AND THE READ-OUT OBLIGATION THIS CREATES:** whichever of (1) and (2) fires
