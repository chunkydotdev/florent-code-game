# PREREG — TESTING THE COUPLING HYPOTHESIS ITSELF (Magnus: "let's test your hypothesis")

**Committed before any data assembly.** Builder s37, 2026-08-14.

## The hypothesis under test (research's, inference-marked at birth)
Screen→live transfer gap is predicted by OPPONENT-COUPLING: planks whose
payoff depends on specific opponent behaviour flatter in self-play (~5-14pp);
opponent-decoupled planks (map/self-knowledge) transfer at face value.

## Design — retrospective arm (free, existing data)
1. **Classification BEFORE outcomes:** every arm with BOTH a screen final and
   live/rated evidence gets classified coupled/decoupled by a MECHANICAL RULE
   applied to its mechanism description only: *"coupled iff the payoff
   requires the opponent to exhibit a specific behaviour (siege, repair,
   ladder, belt layout) rather than being invariant to opponent identity."*
   Research applies the rule to mechanism TEXT with outcome columns hidden
   from the classification table. ⚠ HONEST LIMIT: true blinding is
   impossible — both lanes know most outcomes. The mitigation is the rule's
   mechanical wording plus the side lane auditing each classification
   against the rule text, not against plausibility.
2. **Gap computation:** per arm, screen share minus live share (pooled live
   where it exists; rated break-in delta where legs don't). Report per class.
3. **PREDICTIONS, registered now:** coupled arms mean gap ≥ +5pp
   (screen-flattered); decoupled arms |gap| ≤ 2pp.
4. **THE KNOWN HARD CASE, named before assembly: the SALT family.** Salt is
   coupled by the rule (exploits THEIR belts and repair) yet SHIPPED and
   VALIDATED on the ladder. **If salt reads coupled-with-transfer, the
   hypothesis as stated is FALSIFIED-AS-UNIVERSAL and the candidate
   refinement is pre-named:** coupling only flatters when the exploited
   behaviour DIFFERS between our incumbent and the field; salt exploited a
   field-universal behaviour (belt repair: field 40.5%). The refinement must
   then predict the eco-family gap AND salt's transfer with one rule, or
   the whole frame is demoted to "unexplained divergence, use the haircut
   empirically."

## Design — prospective arm (the real test; predictions before data)
* **GBNS** (coupled by rule): if its screen finals ≥51.33, prediction:
  pooled live n=50 reads ≥5pp BELOW screen.
* **L4REPAIR** (coupled by rule — trigger is opponent belt-cutting): same
  prediction shape.
* **A decoupled candidate is REQUIRED for the contrast** — queue row #8
  (seat-relative scan order: pure geometry, opponent-invariant) is the
  cleanest unbuilt one; if its screen lands outside-above, prediction:
  live transfers within ±2pp. Built as the test's decoupled probe.

## What this test may not do
No ship implications either way (the hold governs). If the hypothesis
survives, the −5pp haircut stays and gains a mechanism; if it falsifies on
salt, the haircut becomes empirical-only and the refinement gets its own
test. Either outcome is banked.
