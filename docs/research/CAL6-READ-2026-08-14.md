**A 32pp gap on Juusto and a 21pp gap on team lazy, between the fixture we call
`FIXTURE_OF_RECORD` and the ladder that actually pays.** Both n's are small and
our version differs across the two columns (panel v140; rated mostly v125), so
this is **not yet a finding — it is the best available question**, and it is what
CAL-7 is redesigned to answer (`PREREG-CAL7-2026-08-14.md`).

**One candidate explanation closed cheaply, with its control: SEAT.** Unrated
challenges are issued by us, so a fixed seat would be a systematic bias. Measured
across 8,338 archived games of ours, seat is balanced in both fixtures and the
seat effect is small: **rated A 52.3% / B 48.5%; unrated A 49.3% / B 46.2%.**
A ~3pp seat effect cannot produce a 32pp gap. **Seat is not the explanation.**
