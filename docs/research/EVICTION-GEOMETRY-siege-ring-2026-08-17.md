# Eviction geometry — where defending builders actually stand when their core is besieged

**BANKED by research s50, 2026-08-17 ~18:1xZ.** Agent report below (verbatim in substance, entities normalised). The largest-ground study of the day: **11,897 qualifying siege episodes / 9,053 with on-core heals / 5,261 games / 79-84 defending teams**, decoded from a seeded 6,000-replay sample of the archive with every instrument guard driven to the other verdict (adjacency law 68,177/68,177 vs 20.2% with tracking disabled; team-mapping 399/399; 0 corner heals of 1,117,509). Cluster-robust intervals at episode level; all headline comparisons stated as exclusions.

## THE ANSWERS, UP FRONT
1. **TWO eviction launchers, and the second is worth more than the first.** Interception of on-core heal traffic (n=1,116,056 events): one launcher = 48.4% strict / 58.5% deny even with adaptive siting (oracle ceiling 68.7/75.8); **two adaptive = 70.5% strict / 86.5% deny** (oracle 92.7/98.0). Marginal gain of the 2nd: +22 to +38.8pp. Three ring-sited launchers close the ring 100% by geometry: (-2,0),(1,-1),(1,2) canonical.
2. **Siting must be REACTIVE, not fixed.** Fixed a-priori best tile = 29.0% (held-out 29.8% — not overfit, just weak; beats blind geometry by only 4.0pp). Watching 5 heals in THIS siege → 48.4%. **Per-episode traffic is concentrated (busiest tile median 55.6%, median 3 distinct tiles used) but WHICH tiles are hot is unpredictable across episodes** — the pooled distribution is nearly flat (max cell 14.58% vs uniform 12.5%). Attacker direction is nearly worthless as a signal (+4pp).
3. **HEALERS CAMP; THEY DO NOT APPROACH.** 94.9%/91.6%/88.6% of healers are already standing on their exact heal tile 3/6/10 rounds before the heal. **An eviction launcher is a remover of squatters, not an interceptor of traffic — any siting aimed at approach lanes targets a phenomenon that does not exist at scale.**
4. **THE DUMP RULE IS WORTH MORE THAN THE LAUNCHER COUNT.** Walk-back (n=12,652 real displacement events, ≥60-rounds-left restriction n=10,655): dumped >5.5 tiles → **33.4% ever return, median 33 rounds**; dumped 3.5-5.5 (= the max-range-from-launcher defect's landing zone, mean 4.0-4.7) → **58.7% return, median 11**; ≤1.5 tiles → 98.2% return, median 1. **Fixing max-range→away-from-core roughly triples dwell and cuts returns ~25pp on the same launcher.** Every candidate placement has a ≥6-tile legal dump on 54-86% of real maps (ceiling structural: none reaches 8). The builder probe's gap-4/~4-victim steady state is the short-dump regime's signature; the long-dump regime is generally available.
5. **SITE ON RING TILES (deny mode): +13-17pp over shell siting** — a launcher ON a ring tile also removes that tile from the heal set outright, AND from the delivery set: **the ring's 8 orthogonal tiles are simultaneously the heal set (0 corner heals /1,117,509), the delivery set (1,524,857/1,524,857 ResourceMoves into footprints originate there), and part of the spawn set** — one body attacks core HP and `titanium_collected` together. Delivery-weighted siting = heal-sited within 1pp on every cell (deliveries are 29.9% of combined ring load).
6. **SPAWN SET IS EXACTLY THE 12-TILE MOORE RING: 59,121/59,121 spawns (100.0000%)** — independently confirms the Jython study. **NW-corner bias 20.28%** (1.7× next tile, 2.4× uniform) — the field's `for d in Direction` loop order showing through; **a barrier/launcher on the enemy core's NW-corner tile pre-empts 20% of their spawns.** REFUTED: "besieged cores spawn away from the attacker" — siege-vs-control ≤1.5pp per side; spawn side is compile-time order, not threat.

## REFUTED (retained): far-side clustering as a siting basis (magnitude too small) · approach-route interception (no approach exists) · terrain pre-concentration (92.9% of cores have all 8 ring tiles open) · threat-aware spawning (≤1.5pp) · "flat pool ⇒ diffuse traffic ⇒ launchers low-value" (killed by the per-episode cut — the flatness is a normalisation artefact and is exactly why reactive beats fixed by ~19pp) · attacker-direction targeting (+4.0pp only).

## LIMITS (the agent's own, kept in full force)
Observational — defenders were NOT being thrown; adaptive figures are an upper bound vs a static opponent. **The partially-sealed sub-case was NOT measured and is the single most valuable follow-up** (seal removes ring tiles → survivors inherit traffic → one launcher's share rises mechanically; decodable from the same rows). Walk-back victims dominated by OUR launcher line (reflects our dump choices). 6,000/61,994 replay sample; meta_join used for attribution only. Dump-reach terrain-only (occupancy reduces it). "Never returned" mixes evicted-for-good with killed (both wins, different phenomena). d²>2 throw detection misses 1-tile throws (the useless ones). Episode definition: hostile within d²≤8 + core damage within 10 rounds, ≥5 rounds, ≥20 HP; control = outside every raw run.
