## Q1 — BARRIER CLEARANCE: THE FIELD MOSTLY IGNORES RING BARRIERS
Field-vs-field ring barriers (n=53,738, 83 teams): **73.5% NEVER damaged**; P≤25 = 21.7% vs far-from-core control 15.9% — **ring proximity buys only +5.8pp of attention**. When paid, the bill is the full solo arithmetic: **median exactly 15 pecks from 1 pecker, ~41 rounds per barrier** (every population). Reactivity control: defender pecks at own ring go 0.1→90.2/1,000 core-rounds when a hostile building appears (~900×), turret shots 3.3→49.2 (15×) — **they react; they under-supply** (90 pecks/1,000 rounds = one per 11 rounds; a barrier costs 167 rounds of that budget).

## Q3 — SPAWN UNDER DENIAL: 126× COLLAPSE, NO ESCAPE HATCH
**262,346/262,346 spawns on the 12-tile ring (engine rule).** Spawns/1,000 core-rounds: 6+ tiles free 25.2 → 1-2 free 8.6 → **0 free 0.2 (126×)**. Affordability control: 44% of fully-blocked rounds had ≥60 Ti banked and still no spawn. **The core does not relocate — it stalls.** Top single spawn tile is the DIAGONAL (−1,−1) at 19.6% (matches the eviction study's 20.28%). Caveat: 36% of full seals are mixed/wall and 6.7% self-inflicted by the defender's own conveyors — "ring full" ≠ "under siege". Reach: 5.1% of core-sides ever hit 0 free; 63 teams observed.

## Q4 — WHO CLEARS A SIEGE, AND HOW
**(a) Cores die to turrets: 94.5% of 23.2M HP of core damage is turret-only** (pecks 2.5%) — independent confirmation of the Jython premise.
**(b) The forward SENTINEL is the most-punished object in the zone: 60.6% damaged / 52.3% killed, median latency 1 round, median life 8 rounds, 80% by turret fire** — vs ring barrier 32.0%/25.3%, latency 4, life 14. **This is the counterplay to design against: LOS/standoff are the levers, not HP.**
**(c) Raider fate (539,265 hostile-builder visits):** 2.8% die; of real pecking raiders (n=8,069) 9.7% die, median dwell 40 rounds/9 pecks. **Field eviction reflex ≈ nonexistent: 1.2% of raiders ever thrown** (best: Hugging Farce 8.0%, SmartFridge 5.3%; most teams 0-1.6%). Highest raider-kill: us 18.2%, Torsko 10.0; Erebus 1.5, Part-timers/Troupe 0.0.
**(d) Seal endings** (hostile-BUILDING stretch definition; the mission's body-inclusive episode definition is UNUSABLE for durations — retained as caution): peak occupancy ≥8 tiles reached at 12.5% of defended cores, full 12 at 1.9%. ≥8-seal median 56 rounds, **66% still sealed at game end**; **full-12 median 9 rounds, 27.4% held — total lockout PROVOKES the response partial lockout does not.** Of 1,235 broken ≥8 seals: turret fire 43.6%, pecks 29.7%, **attacker destroyed its OWN blocker 24.9%** — our seal must not carry that cleanup logic. Per-team ≥8-seal held-to-end: Klarum 94.6%, Bisons 100%, farming_200s 100%, SmartFridge 96 … Besvikomat 27.1, ph 30.5, gsxWins 30.8.

## THE THREE DECISIONS THIS DATA MAKES
1. **SEAL 12, NOT 8** — ~12 extra Ti converts heal-denial into heal-and-reinforcement denial (126× spawn collapse, no relocation escape); the top spawn tile is a diagonal. ⚠ paired with the provocation finding: the full seal wakes defenders (median 9 rounds) — so the value is in completing it EXACTLY when the kill window opens, not early.
2. **BUDGET FOR SENTINEL REPLACEMENT, NOT BARRIER REPLACEMENT** — barriers are 73.5%-ignored and cost the defender 15 pecks each; the sentinel dies in 8 rounds median to turret fire.
3. **TARGET SELECTION DOMINATES EXECUTION** — 0%→88% per-opponent seal survival; cross `target_value.py` with the Q1 per-team column before choosing leg opponents (a seal leg at Klarum and one at Besvikomat are different experiments).
