## Predictions, REGISTERED NOW, and the falsifier

**P1 (primary, powered).** Pooled CAL-7 share on these six cells lands within
**±8pp** of the pooled current-era rated share on the same six (51.3%), i.e. in
**43.3%–59.3%**.
**P2 (secondary, weak by construction).** Per-cell CAL-7 share lands within
±15pp of that cell's rated share on **≥4 of 6** cells. *Per-cell n=25 at the
first look gives roughly ±20pp; P2 is descriptive support, never the verdict.*
**P3 (shape).** The rank order of the six cells by panel share correlates
positively with their rank order by rated share.

**FALSIFIED — the outcome that changes what this project believes — if the panel
reads ≥10pp BELOW rated on ≥5 of 6 cells.** That is a systematic negative bias in
`FIXTURE_OF_RECORD`, and the consequence is written down before the data:
**every published CAL share becomes a lower bound needing a stated correction,
and any ship gate that ever leaned on a panel number gets re-read.**
**The reverse (panel ≥10pp ABOVE rated on ≥5 of 6) is equally a finding** and is
registered as such so the test cannot be read one-directionally.

## Look discipline (declared before data)

1. Descriptive per-cell tallies at any n, plus ob-14 churn counts.
2. **INTERIM look at n=150 (one full cycle): DESCRIPTIVE ONLY.** No verdict
   language, no P1 call. It exists to catch an instrument fault early.
3. **ONE comparative look — the P1/P2/P3 call — at n=300** (two full cycles;
   pooled panel n=300 vs rated n=195 gives ~±7.5pp at 95%, which is what makes
   the ±8pp bar meaningful), **or at the panel's stop if earlier with n≥150**,
   in which case the bar widens to ±11pp and P1 is reported with that bar named.
4. Prior CALs' looks stay spent. `panel_read` always with explicit `--panel`;
   note it currently supports only cal1–cal4, so CAL-5/6/7 are read by the
   fire-log script described in the CAL-6 read (positive control documented
   there: it reproduces s39's published C2 cells exactly).
