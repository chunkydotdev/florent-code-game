## Piece 1 — THE GUNNER PLUG (their best trick, and our leak)
28% of their 1,138 barriers (n=323) are built inside r²≤13 of one of our
gunners; 81% of those eat ≥1 shot (mean 6.6). CONTROLS RUN THE RIGHT WAY:
barriers near an enemy SENTINEL (obstacle-immune) eat a shot 26% / mean 1.6;
no-turret barriers 5% / mean 0.2. Sharpest form: ORTHO-ADJACENT to our gunner
n=242, 1,858 shots absorbed, mean 7.7; they HEAL the rock (2,813 barrier
heals; 1 Ti buys 4 HP vs our 4-ammo-per-7-dmg shot — 2.3:1 Ti edge). Median
plug latency 67 rounds after our gunner's build — REACTIVE, not scripted.
Anchors: 997b672e…_game_1 (archipelago r277 loss): 91 of our 102 gunner shots
(89%) ended on a barrier. 353429e5…: one barrier adjacent to our gunner ate
243 shots (972 ammo) over 248 rounds. e9f8a9fd…_game_5 (royale r301): 42/89.
WE SINK 10,376 ammo into enemy barriers per 180 games — 57.6 Ti/game, 11.9%
of all our ammo, 34.1% of all gunner shots.
⇒ Offensive: raider drops a 3-Ti barrier in a defending gunner's fire line —
cheapest counter-turret in the game, composes with the siege ring.
⇒ Defensive: gunner target selection must refuse to empty the ammo pool into
plug barriers.

## Piece 2 — ZERO CORE-CHEW
0 of 19,198 of their builder attacks target our core footprint; ours: 2,130
of 15,311 (13.9%) for 24 dmg/game into a core that heals +208/game. Their
builder attacks go 78.9% onto our ECONOMY (conveyors/harvesters/bots-on-belt).
Pure reallocation plank: retarget that 14% of forward-builder actions from
core-chew to belt/eco. The core kill stays the turrets' job.

## Piece 3 — COUNTER-BATTERY GUNNER TARGETING
Both sides send ~90% of SENTINEL shots at the core (correct on both bots).
GUNNERS diverge: 19.0% of their gunner shots hit our turrets vs 4.6% of ours
(~2x per-turret after the 1.29x count edge). Payoff: their forward turrets
die at 25%, ours at 48% (n=629 vs 339) despite our 2.7x builder attacks on
turrets. Encasement excluded (blocked-neighbour 1.38 vs 1.31).
⇒ Gunner-only preference "enemy turret in line, else core"; sentinel
targeting untouched.

## Piece 4 — TURRET FIRE ON THE BELT
10.5% of their turret shots land on our economy vs 2.2% of ours. Belt combat
losses 1,424 vs 427 conveyors per 180 games (3.3:1) — our largest combat loss
category by 9x. They rebuild 24% of lost conveyors on-tile vs our 13%; they
demolish spent belt (53 full-HP conveyors, claws back +1% scale each; we: 0).
Ammo income: theirs 630/game vs our 486.
⇒ Gunner target ladder addendum: no turret and no core in line → shoot belt,
never open ground (8.1% of our shots hit empty/own tiles).

## What actually kills us against 0033 (the kill-path paragraph)
We get first blood (their core first hit median r22, ours r59 — a 37-round
head start held in 158/180 games) and lose the race anyway: we deal 7.3
dmg/round to their core, they deal 9.0 to ours, their heal replaces 66% of
what we land. Three leaks, all r60-230, all ammo/uptime, never tempo:
(1) 34.1% of gunner shots into 3-Ti rocks; (2) counter-battery asymmetry —
their gunners suppress our turret line (48% forward-turret death) while one
healed 0033 gunner ground our core 7 dmg/round from r82 to r376 unopposed
(e92cb0a2…_game_3); (3) belt dies 3.3:1 so the answering ammo never arrives.
Their kill path: suppress the turrets, starve the belt, let one healed
forward gunner grind. Ours: arrive first, then run out of ammo.
