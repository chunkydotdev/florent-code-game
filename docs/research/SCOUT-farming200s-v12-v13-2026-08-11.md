## 3. The behavioural diff

Method: `tools/replay_census.py` per-game census + `corpus/build_agg.tsv` +
`corpus/builds.tsv`, joined on the pinned frame. 55 metrics computed; each tested
with a 5,000-draw label permutation, and each re-run as a **within-version
split-half** (v13 early 35 vs v13 late 35, v12 early 27 vs v12 late 28) which
must come out null.

### FINDING 1 — v13 introduced builder-bot attacks on the enemy conveyor line at the enemy core. It is an on/off switch, not a shift.

| population | games | games with >=1 builder attack | total attacks | median/game | % of attacks on an ENEMY conveyor | median d² of target to ENEMY core |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v7 all archived | 10 | **0** | 0 | 0 | — | — |
| v8 all archived | 10 | **0** | 0 | 0 | — | — |
| v9 all archived | 135 | **0** | 0 | 0 | — | — |
| v11 all archived | 70 | **0** | 0 | 0 | — | — |
| **v12 all archived** | **85** | **0** | **0** | 0 | — | — |
| **v13 all archived** | **105** | **87** | **3,329** | **20** | **94.3%** | **2** |
| v13 vs third parties only | 25 | 22 | 1,788 | 23 | 97.4% | 2 |
| v13, pinned frame | 70 | 57 | 1,391 | 20 | 90.8% | 4 |

Pinned frame, permutation p = **0.0002** on both `tot_batk` and `early_batk`.
Within-version split-half controls: v13 early-vs-late p = 0.74, v12 early-vs-late
p = 1.00 (the metric is flat inside a version).

**310 archived games across five prior versions produce exactly zero.** This is
not a distribution that moved; it is a code path that did not exist and now does.

Shape of the raid, decoded per-attack from the replay stream:

* Targets: **1,263 of 1,391 enemy conveyors**, 128 on a tile my tile-map had
  already emptied. **Zero on the enemy core footprint** (`batk_core` = 0.00 in
  both arms, p = 1.00). It is an economy raid, not a core rush.
* Median target distance to the enemy core: **d² = 2** (diagonal neighbour of the
  core's NW footprint corner), min d² = 1. They go for the **terminal delivery
  link**, not the far end of the belt.
* Timing: first attack round min 15, **median 55**, p90 103, max 238.
* Concentration: **1.81 distinct tiles attacked per game, up to 10.8 attacks on a
  single tile** (a conveyor is 20 HP, a builder attack is 2 dmg, so 10 hits is
  exactly one kill), **1.60 tiles per game hit >=10 times.** All three p = 0.0002
  vs v12's zero.

### FINDING 2 — v13 builds fewer builder bots.

| metric (pinned frame) | v12 mean | v13 mean | perm p | v13 split-half p | v12 split-half p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| builder bots built, r0-150 | 7.45 | **5.31** | **0.0002** | 0.90 | 0.61 |
| builder bots alive at end | 5.49 | **4.37** | **0.0002** | 1.00 | 0.23 |
| builder bots built per round | 0.052 | **0.043** | **0.0002** | 0.78 | 0.65 |

Passes both negative controls. Direction is consistent with paying for the raid
out of bot spend (each builder bot is +20% on the global cost scale).

### FINDING 3 — v13 shoots more per round.

| metric (pinned frame) | v12 mean | v13 mean | perm p | v13 split-half p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| turret shots per round | 0.95 | **1.15** | 0.0026 | 0.60 |
| total shots | 154.5 | 235.1 | 0.021 | 0.21 |
| turrets built | 4.24 | 4.17 | 0.87 | 0.37 |

Same turret count, more shooting — a firing-discipline or ammo-conversion change,
not a build-mix change. Weaker than Findings 1-2: the split-half control is null
but `tot_shot` is heavily length-confounded (v13's mean game is 210 rounds vs
v12's 163), so read the per-round row and not the total.

### Metrics that did NOT survive their control (recorded so they are not re-derived)

* **Turret placement distance.** `med_d2_enemy` moves 6.06 -> 10.86 (p = 0.0002)
  — but the **within-v13 split-half also moves it, 12.23 -> 9.50 at p = 0.0006.**
  The metric drifts inside a single version, so the between-version move is not
  separable from that drift. Discarded.
* **First-gunner round.** Mean 27.31 -> 20.79 (p = 0.0078), but the medians move
  the OTHER way (25 -> 29) and the within-v13 split-half moves the mean by 3.0.
  Not separable.
* **Unchanged across the boundary:** first harvester round (median 2, both),
  first conveyor round (median 6, both), sentinel usage (**zero sentinels in all
  415 archived games, every version**), launcher usage (**zero launchers, every
  version**), barriers (zero), splitters (zero). Their build vocabulary is
  core / builder bot / conveyor / harvester / gunner and nothing else.

## 4. What the raid does to the victim (our v104, same 125 games)

This is the *effect* half and it is the weaker half.

| our v104's end-of-game figure | vs farming v12 (n=55) | vs farming v13 (n=70) | perm p | within-v13 split-half p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| titanium_collected | 654.4 (med 580) | **428.4** (med 270) | **0.0060** | 0.86 |
| stacks delivered to core | 65.4 | **42.8** | **0.0060** | 0.86 |
| conveyors alive | 15.6 | 12.1 | 0.066 | 0.45 |
| harvesters alive | 4.6 | 3.9 | 0.088 | 0.72 |
| builder bots alive | 4.8 | 4.6 | 0.40 | 0.63 |

A ~35% suppression of our titanium collection, in games that are *longer* on
average (210 vs 163 rounds), against an identical bot. Permutation-significant
and stable within version.

**But the destruction pathway is NOT demonstrated, and this is the honest
awkwardness of the read.** Counting entity removals straight off the replay
stream:

| removals per game (pinned frame) | v12 | v13 | perm p |
|---|---|---|---|
| our conveyors destroyed near our own core (d² <= 8) | 2.71 | 2.49 | 0.54 |
| our conveyors destroyed elsewhere | 2.31 | 1.50 | 0.18 |
| our harvesters destroyed | 0.24 | 0.07 | 0.03 (**down**) |

**~1.6 kill-capable attack stacks per game land, and our conveyor death count
does not move.** Candidate explanations this data cannot separate: they are
killing belts that were dying anyway; our v104's heal (+4 HP / 1 Ti) outpaces
their 2 dmg / 2 Ti and the belt survives while both sides burn titanium; or the
suppression comes from *occupancy* (a hostile body parked on the delivery tile)
rather than damage. **Nothing here should be read as "they destroy our belts".**

**Map-size control on the ti_collected drop** (same size in both arms):
16x16 706 -> 280 (n 11/10), 18x18 699 -> 263 (n 9/11), 25x25 627 -> 430 (n 3/5),
26x26 913 -> 817 (n 11/12), 24x24 396 -> 383 (n 9/12), 10x10 389 -> 407 (n 9/11).
**The suppression is concentrated on mid-size maps and is absent on the smallest
and two of the largest.** Pooled over common sizes it is 654 -> 429.

## 5. LIMITS

1. **The single largest threat: mechanism and effect are not causally linked.**
   Finding 1 (they added the raid) rests on 105 v13 games with a hard 0/310 prior
   baseline and is about as clean as an archive read gets. Section 4 (our economy
   fell 35%) is a *co-occurring* change in the same games. v13 could have changed
   three other things we have no instrument for — pathing, comms-store use, ammo
   conversion thresholds, target selection — and the raid may be the visible part
   of an invisible rewrite. **We are reading the bytecode of their behaviour, not
   their diff.**
2. **Ladder outcomes and archived behaviour come from disjoint game sets.** The
   +162 rating and 72.5% game share are 120 ladder games we hold **10** replays
   of. The behavioural diff is 125 games we hold replays of, of which **0** are
   the ladder games that produced the rating. Nothing in this file shows the raid
   *causing* the ladder jump; it shows the raid appearing at the same version
   boundary as the ladder jump.
3. **n = 24 matches / 120 games on v13's ladder record.** At K=32 and game-share
   scoring, 24 matches is well inside the range where a real bot's rating can
   swing on schedule alone. The prior-era numbers (v9 +166 at 55.4%, v11 50.9%,
   v12 44.8%) show this team's per-version share moving 10-27pp between versions
   *without* a mechanism as visible as this one.
4. **The pinned frame's opponent is us.** 55/70 of the behavioural games are
   farming vs OpenSverige v104. The third-party check (22/25 v13 games with the
   raid, 97.4% on enemy conveyors) says the raid is not aimed at us specifically,
   but every *magnitude* in section 3 and every number in section 4 is measured
   against one opponent bot.
5. **We do not hold v13's ladder replays.** 10 of 120. Whether the raid is what
   beats Landers/Besvikomat/kladde, or whether those wins come from something
   else, is not answerable from what is on disk.
6. **`meta_join` was used only for behaviour and coverage, never for a
   denominator** — per the standing rule. Section 1b is the demonstration of why:
   it would have reported v12 at 60.0% against a true 44.8%.
7. **The corrupted-input control was run on the headline instrument only** (side
   flip, and five prior versions as a zero baseline). Findings 2 and 3 rest on
   split-half controls, which are weaker.

## An independent check of the number that CONSTRAINS the mechanism

The claim doing the most work here is not the raid — it is *"our conveyor deaths
did not rise"*, because that is what rules destruction out as the pathway and
leaves occupancy / heal-outpaces-damage open. **I re-derived it by a different
path: `corpus/events.tsv` DEATH rows joined to `meta_join`, versus the report's
raw-replay `replay_census` decode.**

| their version | games | our conveyor deaths | per game |
|---|---|---|---|
| v9 | 30 | 227 | 7.57 |
| v11 | 20 | 131 | 6.55 |
| v12 | 70 | 362 | **5.17** |
| v13 | 80 | 310 | **3.88** |

**SAME CONCLUSION, DIFFERENT DENOMINATOR — and the difference is stated rather
than reconciled away.** Both paths agree conveyor deaths **do not rise** under
v13; mine has them **falling**. But the absolute levels differ (5.17→3.88 here
vs 2.71→2.49 in the report), and **the report's number is the better one**: it
pins our side to v104 (55 v12 games / 70 v13 games, our bytes identical across
arms), while **this check pools ALL our versions and therefore mixes our own
changes into the trend.** The monotone fall v9→v13 across MY cut is very likely
partly us, not them. **Units-not-data is this project's dominant defect class;
this is one more instance and it is recorded, not smoothed.**

## What a reader must not take from this document

1. **The raid is not shown to cause the +162.** The report says so and the
   arithmetic is stark: the rating jump lives in 120 ladder games we hold **10**
   replays of, and the behavioural diff comes from 125 games of which **ZERO**
   are those ladder games. **Same version boundary is not a causal link.**
2. **The archive cannot answer outcome questions here at all** — 70/85 v12 and
   95/105 v13 archived replays are our own unrated legs, and archived game share
   reads 60.0% for v12 against a ladder truth of 44.8%. **The report correctly
   took no outcome number from it.**
3. **Two metrics were discarded for failing their own controls** (turret
   placement distance, first-gunner round). That is the document behaving
   correctly and should not be read as the analysis being thin.

## Why this still matters to us

**A live team shipped a builder-melee economy raid — 0 attacks in 310 archived
games across v7–v12, then 3,329 attacks in 87 of 105 v13 games, 94.3% on enemy
conveyors, median target d²=2 from the enemy core, first attack median r55, and
ZERO on the core footprint — and it holds against third parties (22/25 games,
97.4%), so it is not aimed at us.** Whether or not it produced their rating, it
is an existence proof that a rival is spending builder turns on exactly the
denial class this line has queued and never dosed. **And 3,329 attacks bought
exactly ONE conveyor kill** (2 dmg against 20 HP, with healing), which is the
strongest hint in the document that the value — if any — is NOT destruction.
