## 1. THE RECONCILIATION GATE — both tests, and the negative controls

The standing rule: a new attribution path must pass the same seat/winner reconciliation
`join.tsv` passes before any verdict consumes it, because `teamXRating` looked correct for
a day and turned out to be a live join.

| test | result |
| --- | --- |
| **CHECK 1** — meta-derived seat + win/loss vs `join.tsv`, on every file in both tables | **1,445 / 1,445 agree = 100.0000%** (0 seat, 0 winner disagreements) |
| **CHECK 2** — sidecar `scoreA`/`scoreB` + `winnerId` vs the **`winner` field decoded out of each replay binary**, per match | **1,252 / 1,252 agree = 100.0000%**, 0 untestable |
| negative control `--selftest=seat` | **PASS** — flipping the seat on every row collapses CHECK 1 from 1,445/1,445 to **0/1,445** |
| negative control `--selftest=winner` | **PASS** — same collapse |
| negative control `--selftest=tally` (guards the third-party leg) | **PASS** — 1,251/1,252 caught; the single survivor is the one archived match with a **tied 1-1 score**, which is symmetric under a winner flip and structurally uncatchable |

**Both required tests pass at 100.0000%, and all three guards are observed to fail when
fed corrupted input.** A check that reports 100% proves nothing until it has been shown it
*can* report less. The gate is met; nothing below is qualified on attribution.

**Attribution is not hand-rolled.** `tools/corpus/meta_attrib.py` is used as shipped. It
skips the **24 keeper `_meta_stub` placeholders** (`{"id":…, "_meta_stub":true}`, every
other field null) which a naive filename join would have silently attributed to a nameless
team on neither seat, inflating the third-party bucket by ~120 files.

## 3. Q2 — "we run 1.11:1 damage-to-repair against the field's 2.79:1"

**Reconstruction** (verified against the published figure before use): from `econ.tsv`,
`HP repaired = heals × 4`, `damage capacity = ammo_converted × 1.8 + attacks × 2`.
`econ.shots` and `econ.deliveries` are **dead all-zero columns** and are not touched.

| population | side-games | heals/g | HP repaired/g | ammo Ti/g | dmg capacity/g | **ratio** |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **us** (replication; published 1.11) | 1,913 | 431.9 | 1,728 | 758.7 | 1,809 | **1.05 : 1** |
| **field, in our games** (replication; published 2.79) | 1,913 | 247.7 | 991 | 1,407.6 | 2,813 | **2.84 : 1** |
| **field, third-party** | **8,582** | 199.4 | 797 | 735.2 | 1,547 | **1.94 : 1** |
| third-party, side rated ≥1550 | 4,707 | 195.2 | 781 | 815.4 | 1,667 | **2.14 : 1** |
| third-party, side rated <1550 | 3,875 | 204.4 | 818 | 637.8 | 1,402 | **1.71 : 1** |

The ratio is scale-invariant, so the length control changes nothing (per 100 rounds:
field-vs-us 50.6 heals / 287.8 ammo Ti, field-third-party 41.6 / 153.2 — same 2.84 and
1.94).

**Within-team, across the 42 teams with ≥5 side-games in each cell: median change
+0.07, and 20 of 42 go *down*.** So the pooled revision is **composition, not behaviour**:
the third-party pool contains teams that heal more, not the same teams behaving
differently.

**Except for the teams that matter.** The six opponents the late-game doctrine was written
against (`late-game-doctrine-2026-08-09.md` §2) move a long way, and it survives the
length control:

| | side-games | rounds | heals/100r | ammo Ti/100r | **ratio** |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| the six, **against us** | 763 | 454,493 | 23.5 | 351.6 | **7.44 : 1** |
| the six, **third-party** | 600 | 203,470 | 35.0 | 228.8 | **3.20 : 1** |

Per round lived, those six buy **1.54× more ammunition** and heal **0.67× as much**
against us as they do against each other.

> ### VERDICT: **REVISED.** N = 8,582 third-party side-games.
>
> **The field's damage-to-repair ratio with us absent is 1.94:1, not 2.79:1** — 2.14:1
> restricted to sides rated ≥1550. Our 1.11:1 (replicating at 1.05:1) is therefore
> **1.8× off the field, not 2.5×.**
>
> **But the mechanism is composition for the broad field and behaviour for the top six.**
> Across 42 paired teams the within-team median barely moves (+0.07); across the six
> opponents the doctrine actually targets it halves (7.44 → 3.20). **The claim "the field
> spends its titanium on damage" is a claim about the teams we play, and against them it
> is if anything stronger than published — they tilt toward damage specifically when
> facing us.**

## 8. NON-COVERAGE AND LIMITS

1. **The sampling limit is the whole frame.** Third-party games are what the archiver
   downloaded. Nothing here makes them a random sample of the league, and the coverage
   is uneven — 36-107 matches for the best-covered eight teams, single digits in the tail.
   All within-team pairs with <20 games on either side should be read as suggestive.
2. **25 matches / 125 replay files have no usable sidecar** and are unattributed, plus 224
   local arena diagnostics that have no match id at all. 6,244 of 6,369 league-named
   replays (98.04%) are covered.
3. **`opensverige - plan B`** (`b7cafd9f-…`) is a *distinct team id* from OpenSverige
   (`379a5d80-…`) and is therefore counted as field in every pooled row above. Its
   behaviour is indistinguishable from ours (72-76% collar occupancy, 187-300 core heals
   per game against a field median far below that). **If it is a second account of ours,
   the pooled field figures are contaminated by ~100 of 6,244 files (1.6%)** — too small
   to move any headline, but it should be resolved before the next pooled read.
4. **Q3's attacker bins are not fully comparable across populations.** "3+ attackers on
   the core" selects a different siege shape against us (builder melee, which we run at
   1.9× the field's rate) than in third-party games. The adjacency *within* a bin is a
   fair comparison; the bin membership is not.
5. **Q2's damage capacity is a capacity, not damage dealt.** `ammo_converted × 1.8` assumes
   sentinel efficiency and counts ammunition that was banked and never fired. That is the
   published definition and was kept deliberately so the numbers are comparable; it is not
   a measurement of damage inflicted. `econ.shots` and `econ.deliveries` are dead all-zero
   columns and were not used; shot counts, where needed, come from `build_agg` `metric ==
   'shot'`.
6. **Not stratified by opponent version**, though `meta_join` now carries both versions at
   100% of 1,913 of our files. Field bots changed under us during the archive window and
