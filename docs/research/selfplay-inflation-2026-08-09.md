## 2. What the literature says that costs

From sweep 8, verified verbatim against primaries:

- **Agade** (CodinGame, multiple podiums) ran the same amputation test two ways:
  **"~30% in winrate" self-play against "very roughly ~15% seen with CGBench"** — a
  **2× inflation factor, published by a winner on his own headline number**, with the
  stated reason *"self-play evaluation is risky."*
- **Magus** (Spring 2021, ~10th Legend): *"Everytime I tried to add more feature to

- Agade's operational rule from the same source: *"don't waste too much time on
  supposedly 1% coefficient changes that are actually worth 0.1% in the arena."*

> **⛔ NO TRANSFER FACTOR — corrected on the builder arm's challenge, and it was my
> error of the day's own class.** I first published a *"field est (2×)"* column with
> numbers in it. **Agade's 2× is one published figure, for one feature, of one bot,
> in a different game.** The *direction* — self-play inflates, sign flips are
> reported — is well supported and worth acting on. **A column of derived numbers
> gives that single data point the appearance of a calibrated transfer function, and
> we have no measured transfer factor for this game.** The moment a number carries a
> decimal place, someone quotes it. **Fifth instance today of a true statistic
> acquiring more precision than it earned — and the first one both arms would have
> propagated together.**

**Two consequences, and the second is the more important:**

**1. The siting refutation survives.** Its strength was never the magnitude — it was
**dose-response across bands with an exact-zero null band where the gate cannot
fire.** That structure is unaffected by a scale factor. **My recommendation is still
refuted; it is refuted by about half as much as we booked.**

**2. The nulls get *more* null, not less.** A −2.0pp and two −0.7pp results at field
scale are ~−1.0 and ~−0.35 — **inside the noise of any battery we can afford.**
So *"four knobs, none is a lever"* is **strengthened as a direction and weakened as a
measurement**: we did not measure four small negatives, we measured four things
indistinguishable from zero. **The turret subsystem is not slightly harmful to
tune — it is inert to tuning**, which is a cleaner claim and a better reason to leave
it alone.

## 4. What this does NOT license

- **It does not rescue the siting recommendation.** Direction stands on the
  dose-response shape.
- **It does not mean self-play batteries are worthless.** They are the only
  instrument that can run 150 paired games in an hour, and the *sign* transferred in
  Agade's case. What does not transfer is the *size*.
- **It does not apply to the corpus findings**, which are measured on real ladder
  games against real opponents and carry no self-play factor at all.

## 5. The standing rule I would attach to the harness

**Any battery result quoted outside this document should carry its pool.** The
distinction is not pedantry: **a −0.7pp self-play result and a −0.7pp field result are
different claims**, and today five numbers circulated all day without the label.
Agade's discipline is the model — **measure in self-play because it is cheap, then
re-measure the survivors against the field, and publish both numbers.**
