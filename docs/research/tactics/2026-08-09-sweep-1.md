## 1. THE HEADLINE NEGATIVE, and it is worth as much as the positives

**Nobody, anywhere, has documented deliberately inducing an opponent's CPU
timeout.** Three independent search efforts across Battlecode, Screeps,
Generals.io, BattleSnake, CodinGame, Kaggle, Lux, Robocode and RoboCup converged
on the same result. Every timeout passage in the corpus is about **self**-
management. Eight Battlecode postmortems read in full: every bytecode-overrun
story is their own units starving.

That matters for us because we measured (`ammo-and-cpu-2026-08-09.md`) that
Ouroboros discards 26,356 unit-turns across 85 games. **If this works we are
first, and there is no published counter.** It also means no prior art will save
us the measurement.

**Two refinements from the corpus that beat the naive idea:**

- **Aim for the degraded branch, not the timeout.** Top bots do not run correct
  algorithms; they run *truncated* ones with explicit bytecode guards. Battlecode
  2025 "Just Woke Up": bots "would stop scanning nearby mapInfos and proceed with
  their turn". Wololo 2021 replaced a graph search with "a hardcoded triple
  nested loop… truncated to ignore many possible use cases". **A state past their
  iteration cap is much cheaper to create than a full timeout, and the degraded
  behaviour is deterministic and profileable.**
- **In OUR ruleset a crash is worth ~1000x a timeout.** A timeout costs one turn;
  any *other* uncaught exception **permanently destroys that unit for the match**.
  So the state to hunt is the **rare** one, not the expensive one. Precedent that
  rare states kill top bots: 4 Musketeers (2023 top seed) "lost in the winner's
  bracket in qualifiers due to a map exploiting an extremely rare bug in our
  code"; their HQ hit a bytecode wall in a specific map geometry, left friendly
  HQ fields null, and every function touching them threw NPEs.

## 3. TIER 2 — strong evidence, transfer needs a measurement first

- **Legality predicates as remote sensors.** Battlecode 2024 HS winner used
  `rc.canSpawn()` to detect an enemy standing on their spawn pad **out of
  vision**, freeing two of three guard units. Our analogues: `can_spawn`,
  `can_build_*`, `is_tile_passable`, **`can_fire_from`**. *Caveat I am flagging
  rather than hiding:* most of our predicates are adjacency- or vision-bounded,
  so the trick is weaker here than in Battlecode. **`can_fire_from(pos, dir,
  type, target)` is the one worth testing** — it is explicitly hypothetical and
  may not require vision of `target`. One local probe settles it.
- **Turn/spawn-order exploitation.** Gone Fishin' (2nd, 2023) measured **~2/3
  win rate** from spawn *order* alone — outer units spawned first so inner ones
  do not read them as walls. Just Woke Up (1st, 2025) synchronised attacks onto
  even turns "so that when we were attacking enemy towers they would only be able
  to hit one of the soldiers per turn". **XSquare names turn order as a cause of
  side asymmetry — directly relevant to our 7-vs-2 hive seat gap.**
- **Hidden-state inference from aggregate getters.** Om Nom (3rd, 2025) solved
  the enemy's *invisible* building composition from income deltas, because
  income increments were coprime multiples. Our analogue: `get_scale_percent()`
  is a **single global multiplier over live entities** (builder-measured today) —
  so **their scale is an observable of their live entity count**, if we can read
  it. We can only read our own; whether any enemy-scale signal leaks is untested.
- **Baiting a hardcoded panic response.** BC 2019 smite self-reports the bug
  class: spawn-defenders-on-sight caused "defensive units to be overproduced…
  wasting resources and stalling global production". One cheap unit parked at
  their vision edge (sentinel r²=32, core r²=36) triggers permanent overspend.
- **Read the opponent's rule from replays and hardcode the counter.** mlomb
  (Halite III) reverse-engineered a named rival's spawn rule — "he never spawns a
  ship when he have 5 more ships than the enemy" — from replays. **We now have
  the corpus to do exactly this** (`corpus/`, 3,831 replays).

## 4. TIER 3 — tiebreak and endgame, richly documented and directly ours

Our tiebreak is **titanium delivered → harvesters alive → titanium stored →
coinflip**, and the literature on gaming score-based endings is deep:

- **BC 2019 smite won a meta by doing nothing** — "what if we did… nothing? The
  less robots move, the less fuel we burn… Until turn 1000, when their unit
  health would win the tiebreaks."
- **BC 2022 5 Musketeers**: "we could win solely off of having like, 1 gold."
- **Halite II corner-hiding**: stay alive to place above the dead.
- **The counter also exists**: BC 2019 "church lightning" was invented
  specifically to break tiebreak-turtle stalemates.
- **Halite III endgame flag**: switch to pure delivery when
  `distance_to_dropoff x 1.255 > turns_remaining`. **Our #1 key is titanium
  DELIVERED, not stored — so a late hoard scores zero**, and a per-stack delivery
  deadline is computable.
- **Spring Challenge 2021**: price banked resource against score as
  `score + banked/3`, and gate conversion behind a hard round threshold
  (they used day 11 of 24).
- **Cheap tiebreak play**: harvesters alive is key #2 at 20 Ti base — a handful
  rebuilt at ~r990 is nearly free.

**Unverified but cheap to check, and it would steal rather than deny:** our spec
says resources can be pushed onto an opposing team's conveyor network or core,
and conveyors "accept from 3 sides, output to the 4th". If a friendly conveyor at
the end of *their* line can accept their stack and route it to *our* core, that
attacks their #1 tiebreak key and boosts ours simultaneously. **INFERENCE ONLY —
nobody has read the engine on this.**

## 6. NORMS — worth knowing before we build something clever

Robocode argued opponent-specific hardcoding out explicitly: allowed in the
rumble, but "we kind of have a soft ban on pre-loading data… We would probably
also soft ban intentionally building hard-coded Problem Bots for our enemies."
And **budget for a strong exploit being patched mid-league**: Battlecode devs
patched four separate things mid-season in 2025 alone (tower sacking, SRP
overlap, level-2 starting towers, HQ passive damage). 4 Musketeers' response to
the anti-spawn-camp patch is the model: *"Do the exact same thing, but stop when
you're a step away from the action radius."*
