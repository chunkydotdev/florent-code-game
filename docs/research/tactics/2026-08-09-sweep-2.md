# Tactics sweep 2 — wheel topic 11: breaking a structurally defence-favoured line

**Research arm, session 23, 2026-08-09.** Opus subagent, model wired explicitly.
Sources: 8 Battlecode postmortem PDFs (2020-2025) downloaded and read in full,
Screeps forum/docs/wiki, Terminal (Correlation One), StarCraft AI blog, Lanchester.

**The question this sweep was aimed at**, forced by our own heal-arithmetic
finding: *how does anyone break a 2.2:1 defensive edge?*

---

## THE HONEST ANSWER

**Mostly you don't. You win on economy instead — and every league swept
converged there independently.**

Battlecode 2025 / The Kragle, verbatim: *"In RTS games, the counter to static
defense is to disengage and gain an economic advantage since the opponent put a
large investment in static defense."* The StarCraft AI literature agrees: static
defence *"offers initiative to the opponent"* and *"has to pay for itself."*
Screeps, running the most extreme version of this arithmetic for a decade,
resolved it by players simply **not attacking** — *"you can literally build a
nearly unkillable room without a stitch of combat code"* — and the organisers
never patched it.

**Three qualifications, in descending confidence:**

**1. Every league that reached a defence-dominant equilibrium was rescued by a
clock, not by a tactic.** BC2020's finalists wrote that had the tournament *"run
a few more weeks, cookies would be the dominant strategy as defense would prevail
over offense"* — what stopped it was the flood, an exogenous timer. BC2022 and
BC2023 saw matches decided at the 2000-round tiebreaker *"because neither team
could get past all the walls."* **Our clock is round 1000 and our first tiebreak
key is cumulative titanium delivered.** That is a design statement: our game
already names the intended resolution of an unbreakable line.

**2. Our defensive edge is NOT the Screeps edge, and the crack is real.** In
Screeps, repair is cast by mobile creeps with no adjacency limit and rampart HP
is effectively unbounded. Ours is different in one decisive way: **the defender's
heal is capped by orthogonal adjacency at ~16 HP/round per tile (4 bots × 4 HP)
and by the core's one-builder-per-turn spawn rate, while the attacker's damage on
that tile is capped only by titanium.** A linear-law resource facing a
concentrable one. That is where the 2.2:1 breaks, if it breaks anywhere.

**3. The two promising line-breakers both bypass the HP economy rather than win
it**: the Launcher (displacement — see sweep 3, which is the stronger evidence
base for it) and concentration past the adjacency cap (see `sentinel-file` below,
which rests on an unverified engine assumption and must be probed first).

**One negative worth carrying forward:** the wololo-2021 *"drain them by forcing
them to defend"* plan **does not transfer and would actively cost us**. See
`transfers: no` block below.

---

## Tactics

```
---
tactic: Play the tiebreak on purpose when the line cannot be broken
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2022-5-musketeers.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2022 / 5 Musketeers (semifinalists)
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — Verbatim: "If rounds were getting close to a tiebreaker, we would
build a lab and transmute a little bit of lead to get a little bit of gold. Thus,
in a game that was dead even, we could win solely off of having like, 1 gold. It
was sneaky, but it worked... No one could make progress towards the enemy base,
and any soldier who tried died. Lots of games ended in a tie, and we got to semis
(only losing on a 3-2 thanks to our tiebreaker)."
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Closest documented match to our exact situation. Our
tiebreak chain is titanium delivered -> harvesters alive -> titanium stored. Note
the first key is CUMULATIVE, an integral over time, so it is won in the first 300
rounds, not the last 50. The SECOND key -- harvesters alive -- is a snapshot at
round 1000 and IS winnable by a last-100-rounds module: spam harvesters onto ore
near round 950 and survive.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — Our own measurement says median kill round is 296, so most
games resolve by kill; this is an edge-case module, not a core plan. Also needs
enough free ore tiles to matter.
BUILDER HOOK — Two store counters and one endgame branch at ~r940: if no core
kill is plausible, redirect all builders to harvesters on any reachable ore.
Measure the delta on games that currently reach round 1000.
```

```
---
tactic: Disengage and out-economy the static defence
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2025-the-kragle.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2025 / The Kragle; corroborated by StarCraft AI theory
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — See THE HONEST ANSWER above for the verbatim quotes. The three
canonical counters to a turtle: pull ahead in army and bust it, use their
passivity to pull ahead in tech, or pull ahead in economy.
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Our cost scaling makes it sharper than in most games:
gunners/sentinels scale +20% each (a 6th turret costs ~3x base) while conveyors
and barriers scale +1% and harvesters +5%. ECONOMY IS THE CHEAP CATEGORY AND
DEFENCE IS THE EXPENSIVE ONE. A defender investing in turrets pays a steeply
superlinear price for a category that generates no titanium.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — If the enemy turtle out-economies us behind its wall,
disengaging just loses slowly. And our own data (12% of top-tier kills by r100,
median kill r296) says the field is NOT turtling — against a field that attacks,
passivity concedes initiative for nothing.
BUILDER HOOK — Corpus query, no bot change: correlate each team's turret count at
r300 with their final titanium delivered. If turret investment anti-correlates
with delivered titanium in the field, this becomes a real variant.
```

```
---
tactic: Bank the economy, then alpha-strike — partial attacks are wasted
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2020-the-high-ground.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2020 / team Kryptonite; corroborated in Terminal
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — Kryptonite "would often save up a ton of soup as they rushed to our
base, then as soon as they arrived spit out a ton of units." Terminal's version is
the discipline that goes with it: strong algorithms attack only when they
"calculate that it would break through the enemy's defence."
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Exactly right for our ammo economy. convert_ammo is 1:1,
at most once per team per turn, usable the same turn, and DOES NOT CONSUME THE
CORE'S ACTION COOLDOWN -- so the core can bank for 200 rounds and dump 500 Ti into
50 sentinel shots (900 damage) without ever giving up a spawn. Against a ~16
HP/round per-tile heal cap, 900 damage over ~15 rounds is unanswerable; the same
900 trickled over 300 rounds is fully healed and wasted. Our own numbers (heal
4.00 HP/Ti vs damage 1.80 HP/Ti) say every sub-threshold attack is a 2.2:1
donation.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — Banking titanium means not spending on harvesters, which
loses tiebreak key #1 and compounds. And the shooters must be pre-built to spend
through, which is visible and gives 200 rounds of warning.
BUILDER HOOK — A "don't shoot unless it kills" gate: never fire at a building
with >=2 adjacent enemy builders unless >=3 of our turrets bear on it.
```

```
---
tactic: Flank the directionally-biased defence
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2021-wololo.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2021 / California Roll (Chop Suey) — won the Adaptive Strategy Award for it
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — Verbatim: California Roll "sent muckrakers around the edges of the
map to 'muck flank' other turtles which featured directionally biased defenses."
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Our defences are inherently MORE directionally biased
than BC2021's. Gunners and sentinels are facing turrets with line shots. Rotating
a gunner costs 10 Ti plus a full action cooldown -- a third of its own build cost
plus a turn of not shooting. A SENTINEL CANNOT ROTATE AT ALL. So a line built to
face the axis of symmetry is structurally blind on its flanks, and re-facing is
expensive or impossible. Maps are symmetric, so enemy facing is partly computable
from our own layout.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — 8x8 maps have no flank. A defender who builds turrets in a
plus-pattern has no blind axis. Cardinal-only movement makes diagonal flanking
cost 2 moves per diagonal step.
BUILDER HOOK — Read get_direction() on every visible enemy turret, accumulate a
per-quadrant threat score in the comms store, route the assault builder into the
lowest-scoring quadrant. ~20 lines, no new unit types.
```

```
---
tactic: Strangle the economy that funds the healing — deny, don't assault
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2023-4-musketeers.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2023 / 4 Musketeers; and BC2020 field-wide drone harass
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — BC2023, verbatim: "if we send launchers towards these locations,
then the enemy can't get any mana, and they won't be able to build launchers.
This will weaken their defense. Now, we're focused not only on making ourselves
stronger, but also making our opponent weaker." Plus physical denial by "boxing
out" enemy carriers at wells.
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Strongly, because OUR HEAL IS DENOMINATED IN THE SAME
CURRENCY AS THEIR ECONOMY. Heal costs 1 Ti per cast; a titanium-starved defender
cannot heal. And our economy is PHYSICAL -- severing one conveyor strands the
whole downstream chain. That is a THROUGHPUT attack, not an attrition attack, so
the 2.2:1 heal-vs-damage ratio does not apply to it at all. It also hits tiebreak
key #1 directly.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — Conveyors are 3 Ti at +1% scale, cheap to repair if a
builder is near. A builder attacking a conveyor spends 20 Ti of attacks to
destroy a 3 Ti building -- an atrocious raw trade that only pays through the
stranded-throughput effect.
BUILDER HOOK — Corpus query first: measure how many rounds a top-tier opponent
takes to repair a severed conveyor, and delivered titanium lost per cut.
```

```
---
tactic: Chokepoint turret — plus the organiser-patch record as evidence the defensive edge is structural
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2025-just-woke-up.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2025 / Just Woke Up (tournament winners); patch record from The Kragle 2025, wololo 2021, Screeps
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — The 2025 winners built defence towers only where "the tower was in a
choke point, determined by the fact that it had at least 1 wall on two opposite
sides." Their own AB test "did not seem to be very good" and they shipped it on
theory anyway; they credit specific wins to it. Separately, the patch record:
BC2025's devs nerfed defence towers "before sprint 1 even happened"; BC2021 cut
the exposure buff mid-season; BC2020 nerfed vaporators. In Screeps, players have
demanded a rampart nerf for years with no developer response -- and the result is
the acknowledged "nearly unkillable room."
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Defensively the "wall on two opposite sides" test is a
few lines against Environment.WALL. Offensively and more usefully: THE FIELD'S
BOTS WILL WALK INTO IT, exactly as the 2025 winners' own bots did. That is a
pathing bug, the same class of exploitable habit the play-the-players mandate
targets. And the patch record is the strongest available evidence that a measured
defensive edge is real and structural rather than an artifact of our corpus.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — Their own AB test was neutral-to-negative against their own
bots; it paid only against specific aggressive opponents. Matchup-specific tool,
not a general improvement -- consistent with our slot-swap rule.
BUILDER HOOK — Pathing hygiene first: log "rounds spent inside an enemy
attackable-tile set" per match from the corpus. If nonzero we are donating units
to exactly this tactic, and the fix is a router cost penalty, not a strategy.
```

```
---
tactic: Self-destruct / demolish your own to hot-swap a wall segment into turrets
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2020-the-high-ground.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2020 / Bowl of Chowder and Bagger288
evidence: documented
transfers: partial
---
WHAT IT IS — Bowl of Chowder were "the only team to successfully implement
self-destruction of landscapers in order to create islands to build net guns to
break drone walls." Bagger288's "landscapers would self-destruct and be replaced
with net guns if they detected they were soon going to get crunched on."
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — The terrain half doesn't apply, but the HOT-SWAP half maps
onto a rule the field probably under-uses: destroy() is FREE, has NO COOLDOWN, is
UNLIMITED PER TURN, and removes the destroyed entity's cost-scale contribution. So
a barrier line is a RECONFIGURABLE line rather than a commitment -- present a
cheap 30 HP wall for 200 rounds, convert the threatened segment to turrets once
the attack vector is read.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — Build is cooldown-gated while destroy is not, so a swap
leaves the tile empty and passable for at least a turn. If the enemy is already
adjacent, opening your own wall is worse than leaving the barrier. Barrier scale
refund (+1%) is negligible; the real refund is on turrets (+20%), which you rarely
want to destroy.
BUILDER HOOK — None yet; prerequisite is the threat-direction read that the flank
tactic also needs. Build that first.
```

```
---
tactic: Sentinel file — stack obstacle-ignoring shooters on one ray past the adjacency heal cap
source: https://wiki.screepspl.us/Combat/ (underlying doctrine); formation is inference by this sweep
origin: inference by tactics sweep 2, applied to our ruleset; Screeps combat doctrine for the damage-vs-heal framing
evidence: inference
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — INFERENCE BY THIS SWEEP, NO SOURCE FOR THE FORMATION ITSELF. The
defender's heal throughput at any ONE tile is hard-capped by orthogonal
adjacency: at most 4 builder bots can heal a tile, so ~16 HP/round for 4 Ti/round.
The attacker's damage at that tile is capped only by titanium. Sentinels IGNORE
OBSTACLES, so in principle any number can be stacked in single file along one ray,
all firing through each other into the same tile.
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — This is the structural break in the 2.2:1 edge if it
holds. Sentinel 18 dmg, reload 2 => ~6-9 HP/round each; against a 16 HP/round cap
you need 2-3 sentinels bearing on one tile to make net progress, and every one
beyond that is surplus the defender CANNOT answer, because a 5th healer will not
fit on a 4-neighbour tile. Barriers do nothing: sentinel shots pass through them.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — (a) MAX_TEAM_UNITS=50 caps the fleet; (b) sentinel scale is
+20% each so the 6th costs ~3x base; (c) if reload 2 means every 3rd round the
per-sentinel rate drops to 6 HP/round; (d) **UNVERIFIED AND DECISIVE: whether two
sentinels on the same ray can both hit the same tile, or whether the engine
resolves a line shot as "first entity hit" so the rear sentinel's shot is absorbed
by the friendly in front.**
BUILDER HOOK — ENGINE PROBE, NOT A BOT, AND IT GATES THE WHOLE BLOCK: place 3
friendly sentinels in a straight file with one enemy barrier at the end, fire from
each, read HP deltas. If rear sentinels damage the barrier, this is live. If they
hit the friendly in front, refile as transfers: no.
```

```
---
tactic: Force the defender to spend on defence and win the resource race
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2021-wololo.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2021 / Isaac Liao (wololo), section 2.4 economic theory
evidence: documented
transfers: no
---
WHAT IT IS — The most rigorous published treatment of attacking a turtle. Liao's
rush won not by breaking the turtle but by making defence unaffordable: attackers
"forced them to replenish their defenses" and "I then drained the opponent of
conviction since defense was expensive."
WHY IT DOES NOT TRANSFER — **It looks like it should and it inverts.** The premise
is that defending costs the defender more than attacking costs the attacker. In
BC2021 that held. In our game heal is 4.00 HP/Ti and our best damage 1.80 HP/Ti,
so every round we force them to heal we spend ~2.2 Ti to cost them 1. Running
Liao's plan here drains US. FILED SO THE NEXT SESSION DOES NOT BUILD IT.
WHAT SURVIVES — The UNIT-COUNT half does transfer: MAX_TEAM_UNITS=50 and the core
spawning at most ONE builder per turn mean the defender's healer count is
rate-limited by TURNS, not titanium. Every healer permanently removed costs them a
full core-turn to replace, and a core-turn is not purchasable. So attacks that
REMOVE builders attack a resource they cannot buy; attacks that merely make them
SPEND on healing subsidise them.
BUILDER HOOK — None. This block exists to stop the next session building it.
```

```
---
tactic: Overwhelm-the-repair-rate doctrine, and the surface-area breakability criterion
source: https://screeps.com/forum/topic/2809/encouraging-more-combat-at-high-gcl
origin: Screeps, high-GCL combat discussion (player Tigga and thread OP)
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — Screeps is the closest documented analogue to our arithmetic. The
genre law, stated by the OP: "The old military maxim is that an attacker needs
three times the investment of the defender to succeed. In Screeps it is much
greater than that." Tigga names the breakability test: "If the room doesn't have
power enabled, or doesn't have an exposed controller, or doesn't have 15+ rampart
surface area you're not killing it."
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Two things. It independently confirms our 2.2:1 finding is
a GENRE-WIDE STRUCTURAL PROPERTY, not a quirk of our engine -- so it is terrain to
be navigated, not a bug to be engineered around. And the surface-area criterion is
actionable: a defence is breakable in proportion to exposed perimeter, because
heal is finite and must cover the whole perimeter while the attacker picks the
point.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — Screeps repair is mobile with no adjacency limit and rampart
HP is effectively unbounded; ours is 4-adjacency-capped with building HP of 20-40.
Our defence is therefore MUCH more breakable than a Screeps room. The analogy gives
the shape of the problem, not the magnitude.
BUILDER HOOK — Map-size-conditional aggression: split our win rate by map area and
by measured enemy perimeter. If aggression pays only below some threshold, that is
a one-line gate, not a rewrite.
```

```
---
tactic: Displacement — lift the healer off the wall instead of killing it
source: https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2020-the-high-ground.pdf
origin: Battlecode 2020 / The High Ground, describing the field-wide drone harass
evidence: documented
transfers: yes
---
WHAT IT IS — See sweep 3, which is the fuller evidence base. Summary: a heal
screen does not have to be BROKEN, it has to be MOVED, and BC2020's field
converged on exactly that.
WHY IT MIGHT TRANSFER — Displacement is strictly better than killing in our
ruleset, because killing an enemy unit REFUNDS their cost scale and throwing one
refunds nothing. One 20 Ti launcher suppresses roughly one healer indefinitely;
out-healing the same tile by fire costs ~10 Ti per round forever.
WHAT WOULD KILL IT — The launcher must be ADJACENT to the bot it lifts, and it is
a 30 HP immobile building placed inside their kill zone.
BUILDER HOOK — See sweep 3, which has the stronger and cheaper hook (defensive
interception in our own base, where the adjacency comes free).
```
