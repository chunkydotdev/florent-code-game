## The finding, and it contradicts what we shipped

**Four independent league winners/podium finishers encode static-defence value in
their evaluation functions — and in NOT ONE of them does the structure's own
survival appear as a term.** Three of the four make forward-ness explicitly
*positive*.

```
Agade (Code Royale, 3rd), site scoring:
   tower score:  11*(Max_Dist - Dist_To_Enemy(Site))/Max_Dist
   mine  score:  maxMineSize + 1e-2*gold + 2*(Dist_To_Enemy(Site) - Max_Dist)/Max_Dist
```
**The sign flips between the two lines: tower value RISES as distance-to-enemy
FALLS; mine value rises as it grows. Defence forward, economy back** — in a game
with a queen (win condition), mobile knights, and static towers/mines. And his one
self-diagnosed weighting error runs the *opposite* way from ours: `0.25+5e-3*HP` per
tower, *"That is 4.25 queen HP for a fully upgraded tower. This feels way too
high."* — **over-valuing tower preservation.**

**robostac (Code Royale, 1st):** *"Enemy Queens hitpoints / 10. The enemy hp was
divided by 10 **to encourage aggressive tower placement** but discourage trading
hp."* — a winner deliberately distorting his own eval to buy forward placement. And
tower bonus *"scaled based on missing HP squared (this prioritised partially
upgrading towers)"* — **a damaged tower earns more attention than a pristine one**,
the exact inverse of treating survival as the good.

**ryandy (Lux AI S2, 1st of 646):** `('ice_conflict_bonus', ice_conflict_bonus, 500)`
— **the largest weight in the factory-placement function**, above `ice1_bonus` at
300. The winner places its most important static asset into a **contested,
compromised position on purpose.**

**Kaggle Kore 2022, 1st** *(marked `[SUBAGENT]`, single-source, JS-rendered page —
NOT independently verified, do not quote as documented)*: *"An adjacent attack is
intended to die, so it had a different scoring criterium."* **If it holds, it names
our bug exactly: we applied ONE value function to two asset classes.**

## What this says about our error

**Our 86.7% / 19.2% split was never two performances of one asset. It was two
assets.** A home turret is meant to persist; a forward turret is meant to be spent.
**Pooling them under "survival" is a category error, and optimising the pooled metric
removed the one that was doing its job.**

The operational form, from three defensive-engineering sources: Screeps' own docs say
ramparts *"will inevitably fall sometime"*; Winsley, *"The purpose of walls and
ramparts is to protect your core facilities **until** your active defenses can
destroy invaders."* **A structure whose stated job is *until* cannot be evaluated on
whether it lasted.**

## The proposed function — and its honest status

```
R_forward = (enemy Ti spent removing it
           + enemy builder-turns diverted, priced at 30 Ti / bot-lifetime
           + enemy ammo burned on it
           + rounds of enemy economic delay, priced at 2.5 Ti/round)
          ÷ (our build cost + healing Ti + ammo fired)
```
with **`R > 1` as the build gate and NO survival term at all**; home turrets get a
different function entirely.

**Status, stated plainly: the *shape* is supported and the *formula* is not.**
Nobody in any league surveyed validated a defensive-value metric against win rate.
LTD/LTD2's `HP × dpf` and Stanescu's `dmg × currentHP` both make value a product of
persistence and output rate — **neither ever scores individual survival** — but the
specific exchange-ratio above is the sweep's derivation from our ruleset and is
**untested.**

## Actions, in cost order

1. **Free — tag every turret `HOLD` vs `TRADE` at build time and never pool them in
   one statistic again.** This alone would have prevented the −6.7pp.
2. **Free — confirm whether the −13.3pp battery was vs-field or vs-self.** Agade
   measured **30% self-play vs ~15% real** for the same change; Magus reports a
   **sign flip** (*"better in local… but worse in the arena. Overfit nightmare."*).
3. **Cheap — report rounds of enemy economic delay**, already computable from BUILD
   events, and open a proxy-validation ledger.
4. **One decoder pass — add damage/shot/kill attribution.** `econ.tsv`'s `shots`
   column is **zero in all 25,530 rows** (corpus-howto trap 5); until that changes,
   **survival remains the only visible quantity and this error will recur.**
