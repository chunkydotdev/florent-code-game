## 1. Penalise raid stations that lie on a visible enemy gunner's firing line

**SOURCE** — `2026-08-09-sweep-7.md:108-112`:

> "A gunner fires along ONE straight line. A builder
> standing orthogonally adjacent but OFF the facing axis takes zero damage while
> attacking at 2 dmg/2 Ti: a 25 HP gunner falls in 13 turns for 26 Ti and can never
> answer. Rotating costs 10 Ti + a cooldown; a sentinel cannot rotate at all. THIS IS
> WHAT WE SHOULD EXPECT OPPONENTS TO DO TO OUR GUNNERS, AND IT IS FREE FOR THEM."

and the doctrine that makes it a *placement* rule, `2026-08-09-sweep-14.md:108-113`
(quote begins at :108; the rooklift record is on :109):

> "**5. The engagement was chosen so it COULD NOT LOSE.** rooklift's Halite II bot
> went **1279-176** in 2-player rush games by only occupying tiles where *"at most
> one enemy ship can come into range"*, explicitly *"no prediction at all. We prepare
> for what the enemy could do."* Our engine exposes this as a first-class predicate:
> `can_fire_from(position, direction, turret_type, target)` is the hypothetical form
> and ignores ammo and cooldown."

BANNER: `sweep-14` carries a "FORWARD ROAD IS DEMOTED" caveat block at its tail.
It demotes the *forward-plant* road's priority; it does not retract this item.

**FLAG-SIZED DIFF** — `raid.py:499-508` and `raid.py:537-540`. `_raid_station`
already does exactly one pass over `get_nearby_buildings()` to collect enemy
**launcher** positions into `threats`, and already applies one penalty constant
(`LOKI_EXILE_PENALTY = 24`, `doctrine.py:1268`) at `raid.py:537-540`. Collect
enemy **gunners** in the same pass, and in the same place add
`score += LOKI_LANE_PENALTY` when `s` is in
`ct.get_attackable_tiles_from(gp, ct.get_direction(gid), EntityType.GUNNER)`.
One new constant, one extra branch of an existing loop, recomputed only every
`LOKI_RAID_RESCAN = 6` rounds (`doctrine.py:1272`).

**MECHANISM** — raider deaths at the enemy ring go DOWN, so
rounds-of-raider-life-at-the-ring goes UP. Visible as an event count (raider
deaths per game, foothold-heartbeat rounds) before it is visible in win rate.

**PRECONDITION** — **MEASURED, and it is the largest dose in this document.**
`docs/research/builder-death-attribution-2026-08-09.md:64`: of **4,379** US
FORWARD-band builder deaths, **91.94%** are enemy GUNNER, 6.65% sentinel.
FORWARD is defined there as "everything else" outside d² ≤ 32 of our own core —
i.e. it is our raiders. A gunner's kill zone is a single line it cannot cheaply
re-aim, so the penalty has something to bite on. *What is UNMEASURED:* what
fraction of ring stations are gunner-covered at the moment we choose one. That
is a replay cut, not a battery.

**ON-PROGRAMME** — attack. LOKI-6/LOKI-8's own finding (`doctrine.py:1401-1404`,
`doctrine.py:1476-1481`) is that ARRIVAL and establishment are the scarce
quantity, not damage. This buys establishment. It is not screening: nothing is
defended, a raider merely stands on a tile that is not on the enemy's one line.

## 2. Enclose the forward sentinel with barriers and leave exactly one side open

**SOURCE** — `wall-off-the-forward-plant-and-leave-a-rebuild-gap.md:37-41`:

> "An enemy builder's attack requires an **orthogonally adjacent** tile and
> **cannot target builder bots** — buildings only. So a barrier occupying an
> orthogonal neighbour of our sentinel is a literal, rule-level block on reaching
> it: they must destroy the 30 HP barrier first, at 2 damage per 2 Ti = **30 Ti
> of attacks to remove a 3 Ti building**, and barriers scale at only **+1%**."

and the gap rule at `:52-56`, which is *ours* for a different reason than the source's:

> "Liquipedia's open hex exists so *pylons* can be rebuilt. Ours must exist because
> **heal also requires orthogonal adjacency**: a sentinel ringed on all four sides
> is a sentinel our own builder cannot heal. So the correct shape is a **three-sided
> ring with the fourth side facing our approach**"

**FLAG-SIZED DIFF** — `raid.py:267-280` (step 2, "SEAL A FREE SEAT"). Today the
barrier target set is exactly the enemy core's 8 seats: `raid.py:272-273` reads
`if (t.x, t.y) not in seatkeys: continue`. Widen the accepted set by one clause —
also accept `t` when a *friendly SENTINEL* stands orthogonally adjacent to `t`
and at least one other orthogonal neighbour of that sentinel is still free (the
heal gap). The build itself is already legality-gated by `can_build_barrier`, and
`LOKI_SEAL_TI_FLOOR = 12` (`doctrine.py:1210`) already bounds the spend. ~6 lines,
one gate, no new subsystem.

**MECHANISM** — forward-sentinel lifetime goes UP, so cumulative damage delivered
into the enemy core per plant goes UP. Visible as an event count (forward turret
deaths, rounds alive) and then in core-kill share.

**PRECONDITION** — **MEASURED that the problem exists: `CLAUDE.md` records "zero
of our forward turrets died in 480 arena games against 46.9% on the ladder"**, so
nearly half our forward plants die in real games (and the arena fixture is blind
to it). **UNMEASURED: what kills them.** If it is enemy *sentinel* fire the ring
does nothing, because their line ignores obstacles too — the file says so itself
at `:66-67` ("(b) **Their sentinels also ignore obstacles**", `:66`). The instrument is a replay cut of forward-turret deaths by killer
type, the same shape as `builder-death-attribution`. Do that cut before firing
this, or the leg cannot interpret its own null.

**ON-PROGRAMME** — attack. The enclosed asset is the gun shooting their core, not
ours. Note the honest overlap: "sentinel siting forward branch" and "forward
sentinel aiming" are on the burned list — **this is neither**; it changes nothing
about where or whether we plant, only what happens on the turns after.

## 3. Score the launcher throw destination instead of taking the farthest-from-us tile

**SOURCE** — `score-the-throw-destination.md:45-52`:

> "[[launcher-defensive-interception]]'s builder hook is literally *"throw it to the
> farthest passable tile from our core"* — that is **"throw at first good thing"**,
> the exact policy a 2026 team measured itself out of. The scored version costs no
> extra engine call: `is_tile_passable` is already needed per candidate, and the
> destination set is bounded by r²=26 around the launcher."

and the term table at `:56-63`, whose top three terms are `+ tile lies in a
visible enemy gunner's facing line`, `+ distance from their core`, and
`−−− tile is orthogonally adjacent to our core`; builder hook at `:96-99`:

> "replace the destination rule in the launcher plank with a scored
> `argmax` over passable tiles in range, starting with only the three
> highest-confidence terms"

**FLAG-SIZED DIFF** — `raid.py:649`, one line. Today:
`far = sorted(sites, key=lambda t: t.distance_squared(self.core), reverse=True)`.
That implements only the `−−−` term. Change the key to a sum of the two distances
(`t.distance_squared(self.core) + t.distance_squared(dest)`, still `reverse=True`)
and the throw lands in a map corner far from BOTH cores instead of merely behind
us. `sites` is already computed once at `raid.py:624-631`, so there is no new
scan and no new engine call.

**MECHANISM** — enemy builder walk-back rounds per grab go UP. Visible as an
event count (throw destination distance to their core) immediately; only
plausibly visible in win rate at volume.

**PRECONDITION** — **the dose is small and the library says so, quoted rather
than estimated.** `score-the-throw-destination.md:85-88`:
> "**Volume.** At ~1-3 grabs per game at home, the difference between a good
> destination and a mediocre one is a small number multiplied by a small number.
> This is a cheap refinement to a launcher we build for other reasons, **not a
> reason to build a launcher.**"
Our own gating is tighter still: `_try_build_launcher` (`main.py:606-609`)
requires `SLOT_HARVESTERS >= 1` and `LAUNCHER_RESERVE = 80` spare. **UNMEASURED:
how many games we field a launcher at all.**

**ON-PROGRAMME** — denial/exploit. Note the adjacent Loki variant, deliberately
NOT proposed here because the builder already spent it: throwing to a **map
border** tile to induce an off-map query and a permanent unit kill is LOKI-14 and
already lives in `bots/_v131loki14`. This idea is the cheap, non-crash half.

## 5. Never fire a sentinel at a tile one of our own builder bots is standing on

**SOURCE** — `the-blockade-blanks-your-own-guns.md:45-51`:

> "**Turret fire hits whatever unit stands on the target tile, own team
> included** — verified in our own replays, not inferred
> (`docs/research/builder-death-attribution-2026-08-09.md`, and the toolkit's
> validated case: our own gunner #50 shot our own builder bot #3 on **rounds
> 62-89, 13 hits, 56 damage, killing it**, because the bot was standing on an
> enemy conveyor tile in the lane). A besieging builder that steps into our own
> turret's line does not merely block it — **it eats the shot and the ammo.**"

**FLAG-SIZED DIFF** — `main.py:694-700`. The sentinel target scan reads
`bid = ct.get_tile_building_id(t)` and `bot = ct.get_tile_builder_bot_id(t)`,
then at `:696` sets `et` from `bid` whenever the *building* is hostile — with no
test on `bot`. Conveyors are bot-passable, so an enemy conveyor with OUR raider
standing on it is a legal target today and the raider eats the 18 damage. One
condition: skip `t` when `bot is not None and ct.get_team(bot) == self.team`.
The same hole is in the last-resort fallback at `main.py:716-720`.

**MECHANISM** — our own forward-turret kills of our own raiders go to zero.
Visible only as an event count; far too small to read in a win rate.

**PRECONDITION** — **MEASURED, and the number DEFLATES this idea rather than
supporting it.** `docs/research/builder-death-attribution-2026-08-09.md:64` and
`:133`: own-turret friendly fire is **0.33% of 8,664** US builder deaths overall,
and **5 of 4,259** (0.1%) in the home band. This is a correctness fix worth one
line, not a plank worth a leg. Ranked here rather than rejected because it is
free and because 18 damage kills a 40 HP raider in three shots.

**ON-PROGRAMME** — neutral-to-attack: it stops us deleting our own attackers.

lifetime — and **the number that decides both is not in the library**: what kills
our forward sentinels. `CLAUDE.md` has the rate (46.9% die on the ladder, against
