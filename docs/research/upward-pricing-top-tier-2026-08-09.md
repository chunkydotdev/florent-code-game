**G2 is worth reading twice.** Under the flip, CtrlAltDefeat's core-heal rate does not
merely drift, it jumps to 54.20 — because in our own games the flip hands it *our*
rows, and our rate is 57.77. The guard does not just wobble; it picks up the
neighbouring population's signature.

**G3 is the finding of the teeth test, and it is why the rule says "per guard".**
A seat flip is close to a **no-op** in top-vs-top games: both sides are ≥1700, so
swapping them barely changes the band. The gradient survives corruption, therefore
**it cannot certify anything and I do not lean on it anywhere below.** Everything in
§2-§4 rests on G1 and G2, which do collapse. Had I run one guard and reported "the
pipeline has teeth", the untested guard would have been exactly the one covering the
new rating data — the failure mode the rule was written for.

## 6. NON-COVERAGE AND LIMITS

- **The third-party pool is not a random sample of the league.** It is the matches our
  archiver happened to download. Coverage runs 560 side-games (Powered by SmartFridge)
  down to 70 (Focalground) among candidates, and the tail is far thinner. **Every N is
  stated; several cells in §4.1 are N=10-35 and I have flagged each one.**
- **CONTROL B's cells are small by construction.** Top teams rarely play sub-1650
  opponents, so "does anyone our size kill sporks" rests on 25 side-games. **A 0/25 is
  consistent with a true rate up to ~11% at 95% confidence.** The *pattern* across nine
  ≥1900 teams is what carries §4, not any single row.
- **Team-level rating is the median of at-match `ratingBefore` across the archive**, so
  a team that climbed during the window (farming_200s, +150) is banded on its history,
  not its present. §4.3 flags this per candidate; §2's bands are per-side-game and do
  not have the problem.
- **Version currency is a real hole and I could not close it.** The archive covers
  farming_200s at v7-v9, Powered by SmartFridge at v64-v68 of 22 versions, Landers at
  v93-v106 of 6. Checking what each team runs *now* needs the network and this cut is
  zero-download.
- **§2.3's turret counts are whole-game and length-confounded** across bands. The
  launcher *share* survives it; the per-game levels should not be quoted across bands.
- **Collar occupancy at ≥1900 is exposure-controlled (§3.2) but not opponent-controlled.**
  A team that garrisons less because its opponent is lethal would look the same. The
  bot-supply table (§3.3) rules out "they had no bots" but not "they were pinned
  elsewhere" — that is the same strength-conditional trap s20 flagged and I have not
  removed it either.
- **G3 (rating gradient) has no teeth** and nothing in this document rests on it (§1).
- **Not attempted:** ammo economics at the top, first-build rounds on this larger
  sample (s20 measured them at n=20 and they are cheap to redo), map stratification,
  and any per-version cut of a top-tier opponent.
- **I did not run the arena, submit, activate, or edit anything under `bots/` or
  `tools/`. No git commit.**
