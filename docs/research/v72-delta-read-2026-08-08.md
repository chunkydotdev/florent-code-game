**What triggers now.** All of the following must hold in the same turn:

1. Unit is a **builder bot** whose role is `"expand"` (:1701-1702) — not

5. Not converging on a shelled core (:2997-3005 returns).

7. `round - self.relink_rnd >= 8` (:3027, per-unit; `relink_rnd` is read with
   `getattr(..., -99)` and never initialised in `__init__`).

Then it scans `ct.get_nearby_buildings()` for friendly **harvesters**

sets `self.link_queue = self._link_path(ct, _best)` (:3046-3051).

**What `_chain_dead` actually proves** (:3372-3438). It finds one orthogonal

| Terminal condition | Verdict | Line |
|---|---|---|
| reaches a Core footprint tile, or a `CORE` entity | healthy | :3417, :3433 |
| output tile is in-bounds but **empty** | **DEAD** | :3425 |
| output tile holds an **enemy** building | **DEAD** | :3429 |

### Q5 — NEW RISKS (bleed flags, ranked)

The bleed is concentrated vs CAD v117 / kladde v75 / Lunds v45 / Ouroboros —
i.e. **siege, melee-grind and long-game opponents**. Ranked by fit:

**FLAG 1 (top) — the chain watchdog converts expanders into slow repair crews
under melee-grind pressure.** Mechanism, end to end:

- A watchdog relink sets `link_queue` to the **full path from harvester to
  core** (`_link_path` returns the whole route, :3514-3519), not the broken
  segment.
- `_expand`'s move phase then navigates to `link_queue[0]` — the tile
  **adjacent to the harvester** (:3098-3106), i.e. the *far* end from a
  core-side break.
- `_build_next_link` can only inspect/pop a tile it is orthogonally adjacent to
  (:3581-3582), so the builder **re-walks the entire existing chain at roughly
  one tile per round**, popping occupied tiles, before it reaches the hole.
- The trigger condition is `not self.link_queue` (:3026) — which is exactly the
  state of a builder that was free to go find new ore. **The watchdog
  systematically preempts expansion.**
- His own chain-medic comment (:2903-2922) measures the input rate: vs
  kladde_probe, ~70% of economy-building damage was enemy builder melee, 376
  hits clearing **~37 conveyors per game**. Each cleared conveyor near a
  harvester is now a candidate relink. **A melee-grind opponent buys N rounds
  of an expander's time for 10 swings (20 Ti) per conveyor.**
- Multiple idle expanders in vision of the same convicted harvester all take
  the job (the comment at :3023-3024 concedes duplicates and calls them
  harmless — harmless for the *build*, not for the *labor*).

  **Directly exploitable, and it is a denial row:** kill one conveyor at the
  **core end** of his longest chain. He replans from the harvester end and
  spends the chain's whole length in walking rounds before repairing. Repeat.

**FLAG 2 — v71's screen barrier can manufacture v72's DEAD verdict, and
`_link_path` then blocks the trunk.** `_try_screen` plants barriers on the
threat→core ray. It excludes walls, ore (new), and tiles within manhattan 1 of
a core footprint tile (:2539) — but **not** tiles that are the output tile of
an existing trunk conveyor. A barrier there genuinely kills the chain, so
`_chain_dead` correctly returns True at :3418; then the new `_link_path` clause
(:3471-3485) marks that trunk conveyor's tile as **blocked**, so the replan
must route a **parallel chain around its own trunk**. Every extra conveyor is
+1% team-wide cost scale — the "money→labour" chain his own comment (:2908-2911)
says pinned the bank under the respawn floor for ~600 rounds. This fires
**only under siege**, i.e. precisely against the 1600+ band. He never calls
`destroy()` on the offending barrier, which is free.

**FLAG 3 — two whole-map BFS in one unit-turn.** `_link_path`'s decoded-map
reverse BFS (:3490-3511) now runs from a third, far more frequent call site
(every ≥8 rounds per idle expander that convicts something), and `_expand`'s
move phase immediately after calls `_nav` → `_bfs_direction`, a second full BFS
(:3665-3756). Both have `_cpu_exhausted` checks every 64 steps, so this
degrades rather than TLEs — but the degradation is **the move being dropped on
the round the replan happened**. On 25x25-30x30 maps this is a new, repeating
cost that did not exist in v69.

**FLAG 4 — `endgame_dumped` suppresses the magazine drip even when it converts
nothing.** :889-899 sets the flag on *turret visibility*, before knowing
whether `amt > 0`. From r960, if `ammo >= cap` the bot converts nothing **and**
the ordinary 16/turn top-up is suppressed for that turn (:968). Since
`cap = (1000 − rnd)·(3g + 5s)` shrinks to ~3-5 by r995, the last ~10 rounds are
a hard ammo standstill. This is our own piece and our own risk too — but if his
r960-1000 window is where a 1600+ opponent finishes him, it belongs on the map.

**FLAG 5 — the r960 economy-reserve collapse.** In `_expand` the endgame branch
bypasses **`_eco_spendable`** (:2882-2886), which is the `SIEGE_HEAL_RESERVE_TI`
gate (:1117-1141) that exists to keep a till for heals/pecks/counterbattery
under siege. From r960 an expander will spend the bank to zero on scaled
harvesters (+5% each) **while under siege**. Again our piece, but against a
CAD-class opponent still pushing at r960 this is a real last-40-round defensive
spend collapse.

**FLAG 6 (low) — `_chain_dead` false-positive on fresh harvesters.** :3393-3394
convicts any harvester with no orthogonal friendly conveyor and full
neighbour visibility. A just-built harvester is exactly that for the rounds
before its first link lands. Cost is a wasted scan + a duplicate plan, not a
regression, but it raises the watchdog's duty cycle in the early/mid game.

**Hunk 12, `_link_path` dead-trunk exclusion** (:3471-3485). Adds an `else`
