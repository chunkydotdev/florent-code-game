## Verdict

**v78 is neither of the two hypotheses on the table.** It is a **parallel branch off `opp_v68`** — a sibling of the v69→v72→v74→v76 chain, not a descendant of it — carrying **39 hunks, 229 lines added, 100 removed, 3 new functions and 0 removed functions** relative to v68. Its content is three planks: a **verified-path-delay rewrite of the S4 choke walls ("S5")**, a **launcher-heartbeat fix**, and a **live forward-gun counter** replacing a monotonic one. Everything else is v68 bit-for-bit.

**PRIME HYPOTHESIS: REFUTED, twice over.**

1. *Not* the `_live_nests` / core-grind-gate fix. v78 contains **no nests machinery at all** — no `_live_nests`, no `CORE-GRIND GATE`, no `SLOT_ENEMY_HP` (slot 5 is explicitly freed in v78), no `_raider`, no `_ferry_launcher`. The defect we routed to x3r0 lives on a branch v78 is not on. The routing did not land here.
2. *Not* a `_v70mh` revival either. The redirect's evidence — the docstring's `"_v70th variant: TURRET-HUNTING UNDER SIEGE … bit-for-bit _v70mh"` block — is **inherited boilerplate present verbatim in every one of `opp_v67, v68, v69, v72, v74, v76, v78`** (`grep -c` = 1 in all seven). It carries no version-discriminating information. Measured distance confirms: v78→`_v70mh` is 1856 changed lines, the *second worst* of nineteen bases tested; v78→`opp_v68` is 341. The docstring lagged exactly as the redirect warned it might.

**The one genuinely new banner block is "S5", and it is provably dead code.** I verified this independently, twice: (a) the planner gate `len(self.map_walls) >= ORE_STEPOFF_MIN_WALLS` (=80, `:121`) admits **4 of the 33 embedded map entries**; (b) on all 8 anchor-orientations of those 4, the best achievable path delay is **4, 0, 0, 2** against the new acceptance threshold `CHOKE_MIN_PATH_DELAY = 8` (`:149`). My candidate search is a strict *upper bound* on the planner's (I scan every on-path tile; the planner additionally applies a local-width filter), so `self.choke_tiles` is **empty for the entire match on every map in the pool**, and `_try_choke_wall` (`:3134`) returns `False` on its first guard every round forever. The headline feature of the upload does nothing.

The far more consequential fact is **what branching from v68 costs**: v78 sheds every E-series fix that landed on the v72 branch — ore-pave ban, melee-futility ledger, `_chain_dead` chain watchdog, dead-trunk detection, scarce-ore widening, Piece-H endgame spend-switch, peacetime ammo floor, and the never-wall-an-ore-tile screen check. That is a large, well-documented regression surface reintroduced wholesale.

**Irony, and it survives the read:** the "turret-hunting under siege" plank the docstring advertises — a designated builder pecking the turret that is shelling the core instead of healing through it — *is* an incoming-side answer to the CAD-family incoming-deficit channel the Eir 8 read named, and it is a variant of what we queued as the E1 design pass. But it is **not new in v78**: it is present identically in v68 and in every version on both branches, including our own live v77. x3r0 did not independently ship it here; both lines inherited it from the shared ancestor. The graft ledger gains nothing in either direction from this upload.

## Risk scan — ranked

The upload is unnamed and mid-window, but it is **not sloppy code**. The crash surface is empty; the risk is strategic regression, not haste.

### R0 — Unit-death risk: NONE (structural)

`run()` (`:753`) wraps its sole entry `_dispatch` in a bare `except Exception`. `GameError` subclasses `Exception` (`.venv/.../fcode/_types.py:9`), so every vision-edge getter and every illegal mutating call is swallowed. **No v78 unit can be permanently destroyed by an exception.** All 34 mutating call sites were enumerated and every one is immediately guarded by its matching `can_*`. Do not plan an exploit around unit death. Static undefined-name scan clean; the two deleted slot constants have no dangling readers.

### R1 — HIGH: siphon exposure, unmitigated on four independent paths

The engine's output rule is team-blind, so an unwired harvester adjacent to an enemy belt feeds it. v78 defers all wiring: the harvester build sites (`:2532`, `:2741`) only *compute* a tile list via `_link_path` (`:3222`); actual conveyors are laid one per ~2 rounds by `_build_next_link` (`:3341`, requires Manhattan distance exactly 1, and build/move are mutually exclusive). Exposure paths:

| path | line | duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| normal deferred wiring | `:2543` / `:2748` → `:3341` | ~2×path_len rounds, **every harvester** (L=6-10 ⇒ 12-20 rounds ⇒ 4-6 orphan stacks, 40-60 Ti) |
| 2nd harvester built while a queue is pending (`if not self.link_queue`) | `:2541`, `:2746` | **permanent** |
| linker builder dies mid-chain (per-unit `link_queue`, `:646`) | — | **permanent** |
| chain link destroyed later — **no `_chain_dead` in v78** | — | **permanent** |

Worse, `_build_next_link:3351` pops any planned tile that has a building on it **with no team or type check** — if an enemy conveyor sits on a planned tile, v78 wires its own trunk *into the enemy belt*. v72 shares this bug but detects it via `_chain_dead:3427` (`if ct.get_team(nid) != self.team: return True`) and re-links. **v78 cannot.** There is also zero enemy-belt awareness in harvester siting (`_pick:3396` sweeps the whole map by distance from our core; `_harvester_site_safe:2061` checks only enemy *gunner* facing rays, exempts the first harvester, ignores sentinels). With `ECO_CAP = 18` it will walk into enemy territory and build beside the enemy trunk. *(Round arithmetic inferred from the cooldown model, not replay-measured.)*

### R2 — HIGH: self-inflicted ore freezing (no ore-pave ban)

`PAVE_TRAIL_ON = True` (`:401`). Both pave branches in `_move` (`:3573` Piece-F trail, `:3593` legacy) gate on `ct.is_tile_empty(pp)` only — an unbuilt ore tile passes. **There is no `ORE_TITANIUM` test in either branch**; the 5 `ORE_TITANIUM` hits in the file are map decode, ORE STEP-OFF movement, retarget, and `_pick`. v72 has exactly this gate at `:3805` and `:3839` (fails *closed* on unreadable env). Once paved, `_build_next_link:3351` treats the tile as correctly occupied and `_pick:3418` skips it — the harvester site is dead for the match. v78's own comment (`:3552-3557`) documents the mechanism and patches it with a **hardcoded 25×25 `hive_map` exclusion** rather than the general ban. `_try_screen:2404` adds a second freeze source: v72's "NEVER WALL AN ORE TILE" check (v72 `:2531`) is absent, so screen barriers can land on ore. **Piece F pave ancestry IS present in this line** (`:363` block) — so the misroute channel is open, with none of the hygiene.

### R3 — MEDIUM: the headline feature is inert, and the fallback was removed

Per the verdict: `choke_tiles` never populates. v68 *did* build chokes on the 4 wall-heavy entries (accept-first-pinch). Those barriers added 0-4 steps of delay — near-worthless, so S5's *reasoning* is correct — but v78 now pays the planning BFS (measured 1.7-2.5 ms on the interceptor's first turn; 2450 nodes on 26×26/walls=208) and ships nothing. Net effect ≈ neutral-to-slightly-positive on titanium (saves ≤9 Ti of dead barriers), strictly negative on CPU. **The author cannot have measured this**: their own comment (`:458-461`) notes `get_cpu_time_elapsed()` reads 0 under local `fcode run`, so the CPU guard never fires locally and the planner always completes — still producing nothing.

### R4 — MEDIUM: reverted E-series fixes (regression surface, not a bug)

Absent from v78, present in v72, each with its own forensic justification on our books: Piece-H endgame spend-switch + `_core_turret_mix` (no r960 ammo dump, no eco-cap release ⇒ tiebreak-2 losses return); ORE PAVE BAN (R2); SCARCE-ORE WIDENING (the infinite 3-turn ore-squat move loop returns on ≤8-ore maps); MELEE FUTILITY LEDGER (bait-barrier drain, measured 8:1 Ti exchange, returns); `_chain_dead` (R1); dead-trunk detection in `_link_path`; peacetime ammo floor — **`ti_floor` drops 46 → 12** (`:868`), re-enabling the ammo-milking that pinned the bank below harvester rebuild price.

### R5 — LOW: store slots clean; two pre-existing launcher races survive

All 14 live slots distinct, all < 16, no encoding collisions (`round+1` heartbeats vs `pack_pos` bitpacks never share a slot). Two races, **both inherited from v68, neither new**: `_try_build_launcher`'s docstring (`:1017`) claims *"Claim BEFORE build so later units this round skip"* — **false under buffered-write semantics**, two defenders promoted in the same round both pass `_launcher_alive` and both build; `_offer_launch` (`:1773`) has the same shape with `chosen == 0`. One new latent hazard, unproven: `:1037` writes `SLOT_LAUNCHER, 0` on build failure, which could in principle land on a live heartbeat and cause a 1-round `_launcher_alive` flicker; the `:1020-1023` rescan closes it in practice and I could not construct a reachable case.

### R6 — LOW: TLE

`_terrain_path_length` is called from exactly one site (`:1247`) inside the once-per-match decode branch — max 6 calls, interceptor's first turn only, not a hot path. The 64-node CPU guard (`:3121`) is adequate (~1 µs/node vs 2000 µs headroom between `CPU_BUDGET_US = 8000` and the 10 ms kill). `_sync_forward_guns:1789` calls `is_in_vision` outside its try, costing at most one lost turn. No unbounded loops introduced.

## Ladder implication

**Expected: worse than v76's −8.8/8, and materially worse than the v77 window it displaced — but with wide uncertainty and one real upside.** The arithmetic is unfavourable on three counts. First, v78's headline feature is inert, so the upload buys **zero** of what it advertises; the two genuine fixes (launcher heartbeat, live gun count) are real but narrow — they unblock rebuild loops that were stuck, which is worth something on long games and nothing on short ones. Second, and dominant: branching from v68 **discards seven measured fixes** that the v72 branch earned, including the ore-pave ban and the chain watchdog. Those were not speculative — they were patched in response to forensically decoded losses, and v78 reintroduces every one. The `ti_floor` 46→12 regression alone re-opens the ammo-milking channel we know pins the bank below harvester rebuild price. Third, v78 is *more* exposed to the siphon channel than any version on the ladder, with four independent orphan paths and no re-link, against a field that has been getting better at belt adjacency. Against that: the turret-hunting plank does address the CAD-family incoming-deficit channel, and it is genuinely the right shape of answer — but it is inherited, not new, so it was already in the v76 baseline that scored −8.8/8, and it cannot explain a swing in either direction. Net read: **v78 should underperform v76**, and the swap out of v77's +20.2/5 window is very likely value-destroying. The honest uncertainty is that no version on either branch has been measured against the *current* field, so the −8.8 baseline is stale; and the two fixes are in subsystems (launcher rebuild, forward-battery re-arm) whose production frequency we have never instrumented — H6 and H3 are the cheapest ways to convert that unknown into a number. **Recommendation: let the benchmark (osb+sh vs opp_v78, 240/tag, running) decide, and register H1 first** — it is a zero-cost falsifiable prediction that validates or breaks this entire read in one pass.
