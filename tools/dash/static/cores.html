What is running locally

Read-only. Files and ps only — no fcode calls, no network, nothing that can submit or spend a rate-limited request.

Every number here carries its age. A value past its own refresh cadence is marked STALE, and a source that could not be read is BLIND — never silently zero. ship_watch once printed a healthy-looking line off seven-minute-old rows, and a healthy line and a blind line were byte-identical. That is what these badges exist to stop.
